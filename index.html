Put this planner on your site

For trlibrary.com editors and partners. Paste into a Drupal Full HTML block (or any web page). The planner sizes itself to fit.

<div data-trnp-planner></div>
<script src="https://trnp-planner.labs.trlibrary.com/embed.js" async></script>

Optional settings on the div:

AttributeWhat it doesExample
data-fromStarting point picked on load: library, south, north or i94data-from="i94"
data-timeMinutes picked on load: 90, 120, 180, 240, 360 or 540data-time="240"
data-min-heightStarting height in pixels before the planner reports its sizedata-min-height="900"

Links work too: trnp-planner.labs.trlibrary.com/?from=i94&t=120 opens the two-hour plan for I-94 travelers. Add &utm_source=… for campaign tracking.

Where this comes from. Park stops, photos, visitor center hours and alerts: National Park Service API . Library entry times: the Library's ticketing system. Drive times: OpenStreetMap roads via OSRM, plus a 10% cushion for park roads and wildlife. Map tiles: USGS The National Map.

Roads and trails change with the weather. Check current park conditions before you go. The North Unit runs on Central Time; times here are Mountain Time.
